How We Started

Let's be honest: being a teen right now is tough.

Between academic pressure, social media anxiety, and the lingering effects of a disconnected few years, finding a genuine community isn't as easy as it used to be. A lot of kids feel like they're the only ones sitting alone, when in reality, so many of their peers are feeling the exact same way.

That's why we launched Youth Connect CLE, a Cleveland-based initiative dedicated to strengthening teen social health. We wanted to bridge the gap between teens who want friends and the incredible local clubs, groups, and hobbies scattered across our city.

We are proud to be inspired by and aligned with the Pittsburgh Social Health Initiative, exploring community-driven solutions to social isolation and measuring the impacts of connection on public health.

Think of us as the ultimate community matchmakers.

Our mission is simple: get teens together.

We're dedicated to helping Cleveland's youth build meaningful relationships through shared interests and safe environments. No algorithms, no filters. Just real people finding their thing.

Accessible to Everyone

The Find Your Thing Fair is a completely free connection fair for teens across greater Cleveland. We ensure you have a place to show up without barriers—including easy accessibility via RTA public transit right to the CPL Downtown.

Interest-Driven Connections

Whether you are looking for your people, your passion, or your next opportunity, this is the place to find it. We connect you with incredible local organizations to match your vibe.

Safe and Supportive

We help teens find familiar, welcoming 'third spaces' that foster belonging. Research shows that regular presence in these spaces creates familiarity and increases adolescent social well-being.
