The Surgeon General said it
In 2021 and 2023, the U.S. Surgeon General issued back-to-back advisories declaring teen loneliness a public health emergency.
Technology changed things
Social media simulates connection while quietly replacing the effort real connection requires. Digital proximity is not the same as belonging.
But connection can be built
Research shows loneliness is not permanent. Connection can be built intentionally, in the places teens already are, with the right spaces and people.
Our Story

It started on a playground swing.

During the pandemic, our founder Eliana Fieldman felt profoundly alone. She was sitting on a swing at the playground when she found out school had shut down for three days. Three days turned into two weeks. Two weeks turned into a year.

When schools reopened, something settled. But Eliana noticed that for most teens, the disconnection did not end when the restrictions did. In many ways, it deepened. And technology made it harder to recover.

Loneliness taught me that connection is not passive. It is something we practice.

She started reading about social health almost obsessively. What she found was clear: teens are the loneliest generation on record, and the systems around them are not built to fix it. So she decided to build something herself.

Youth Connect CLE is the result. A nonprofit dedicated to helping Greater Cleveland teens find their people, their interests, and their place in this city. Starting with the Find Your Thing Fair this fall.

Who We Are

Built for Belonging.

Youth Connect CLE is a Cleveland-based nonprofit dedicated to strengthening teen social health. We connect young people to organizations, opportunities, and each other.

You don't need to change who you are to find your people. You just need a place to connect.

Our work is inspired by the model of Pittsburgh Social Health, which centers social connection as a vital part of well-being. We believe social health deserves the same attention as physical and mental health.

Social Health First
Connection is a protective factor. When teens feel seen and included, they thrive in every area of life.
Cleveland Proud
We work across Greater Cleveland, connecting teens across schools and neighborhoods that rarely get to meet.
Interest-Driven
Shared interests are shared ground. We use what teens already love as the on-ramp to community.
Research-Backed
Everything we do is grounded in the science of social connection and adolescent well-being.
