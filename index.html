About Us

A teen-led nonprofit, built on real research.

Youth Connect CLE was founded and is led by Eliana Fieldman, a Cleveland-area high schooler who spent two years researching adolescent isolation. That research became The Practice of Belonging, and it shapes everything we build, from the Find Your Thing Fair to every program on our roadmap.

Our mission is simple. Get Cleveland teens together in person, around things they actually care about. No algorithms, no filters. Just real people finding their thing.

Want to go deeper? Read our connection toolkit or the Surgeon General's report on the loneliness epidemic.

What's Next

Beyond the fair.

The Find Your Thing Fair is just the start. Here's what we're building next, all teen-led and teen-founded.

In Development

Club Seed Fund

Micro-grants ($50–$150) to help teens launch their own clubs and neighborhood connection projects, from a pickup basketball game to a board game night. We supply the funding and a Connection Resource Kit, they handle local execution. We're seeking a foundation sponsor to get this off the ground.

In Development

Peer Facilitator Corps

A core group of teen ambassadors trained as connection facilitators for other Cleveland nonprofits. When partner organizations host events, our facilitators deploy icebreakers, prompt games, and human bingo cards, building our network's presence while supporting community partners.

In Development

Teen Storytelling & Podcast Series

A monthly spotlight series, published via Substack, Spotify, or short-form video, translating our research into accessible stories, featuring local youth leaders, artists, and organizers sharing their connection journeys, mental health tips, and local impact stories.

We're also building a Youth Advisory Board and are in the process of filing for official 501(c)(3) nonprofit status.
